Recipe of Butter Chicken

Overview of the Butter Chicken Making Process

1. First, take fresh chicken pieces and wash them properly with clean water. Remove excess water and keep the chicken aside in a bowl.

2. Marinate the chicken using curd, turmeric powder, red chili powder, salt, ginger-garlic paste, and lemon juice. Mix well and leave it for 1–2 hours for best taste.

3. Heat a pan or grill and cook the marinated chicken until it becomes slightly golden and cooked. Keep it aside after roasting.

4. In another pan, heat butter and add chopped onions. Fry until golden brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until raw smell disappears.

5. Add tomato puree and cook until it becomes thick and smooth. Stir continuously until oil separates.

6. Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, garam masala, and salt. Mix well and cook the masala for a few minutes.

7. Add the cooked chicken pieces into the gravy. Mix well so the chicken absorbs all flavors.

8. Add fresh cream and a little butter for rich taste. Simmer on low flame for 5–10 minutes.

9. Finally, garnish with coriander leaves and a little cream swirl. Serve hot butter chicken with naan, roti, or rice.
