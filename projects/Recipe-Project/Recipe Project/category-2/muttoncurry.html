Recipe of Mutton Curry

Overview of the Mutton Curry Making Process

1. First, take fresh mutton pieces and wash them properly with clean water. Remove all excess water carefully. You can add a little turmeric while washing for cleanliness. Keep the cleaned mutton aside in a bowl.

2. Marinate the mutton using turmeric powder, chili powder, salt, curd, and a little ginger-garlic paste. Mix everything well and leave it for 1–2 hours for best taste and softness. Marination helps the mutton absorb spices deeply.

3. Heat oil in a kadai or deep pan on medium flame. Add curry leaves and green chilies. Let them splutter and release aroma. This forms the base flavor of the curry.

4. Add finely chopped onions and fry until golden brown. Stir continuously so they cook evenly. Properly fried onions give rich taste and thickness.

5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until raw smell disappears.

6. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft and mushy. Stir until oil separates from the mixture. This creates a rich masala base.

7. Add turmeric powder, chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt. Mix well and fry the masala for a few minutes on low flame. Spices give color and strong flavor.

8. Add the marinated mutton pieces into the masala. Mix well so all pieces are coated evenly. Cook for 8–10 minutes to seal the flavors.

9. Add enough water and close the lid. Cook on low flame until mutton becomes soft and tender. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking.

10. Finally add garam masala and chopped coriander leaves. Simmer for 2–3 minutes and switch off the flame. Serve hot mutton curry with rice, roti, or naan.
