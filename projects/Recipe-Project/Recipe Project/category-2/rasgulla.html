Recipe of Special Rasgulla

Overview of the Special Rasgulla Making Process

1. First, boil fresh full cream milk in a large vessel while stirring continuously so the milk does not burn at the bottom. Once the milk starts boiling properly, add lemon juice or vinegar slowly while mixing gently. The milk will begin to curdle and separate into soft paneer and water. Turn off the flame immediately and strain the mixture using a clean cloth. Wash the paneer with cold water to remove the sour taste completely and squeeze out excess water carefully.

2. Hang the paneer for some time so the extra water drains properly while still keeping the texture soft and moist. Transfer the paneer onto a plate and knead it gently using your palms until it becomes smooth, soft, and slightly creamy. Proper kneading is very important because it helps rasgullas become spongy, soft, and juicy after cooking.

3. Divide the kneaded paneer into small equal portions and roll them carefully into smooth round balls without any cracks. Cracks may cause the rasgullas to break during boiling, so shape them gently with light pressure. Make sure all the balls are equal in size for even cooking and attractive appearance.

4. In another deep pan, prepare sugar syrup by boiling water and sugar together while stirring continuously until the sugar dissolves completely. Allow the syrup to boil properly because rasgullas need enough space and heat to expand while cooking. Add cardamom powder or rose essence for a pleasant aroma and rich flavor.

5. Slowly add the prepared paneer balls into the boiling sugar syrup and cover the pan with a lid. Cook them on medium flame for several minutes because the rasgullas will expand in size while absorbing the syrup. Proper cooking makes them soft, fluffy, and spongy from inside.

6. Once the rasgullas become soft and double in size, turn off the flame and allow them to rest in the sugar syrup for some time. Resting helps the sweetness spread evenly inside the rasgullas and keeps them juicy for a longer time.

7. Transfer the rasgullas carefully into serving bowls along with the syrup. Garnish with saffron strands, chopped pistachios, or rose petals to make the dessert look rich and attractive. The soft texture and sweet aroma make rasgullas extremely delicious and refreshing.

8. Finally, serve the special rasgullas chilled or at room temperature during festivals, celebrations, and family gatherings. Their juicy texture, rich sweetness, and soft spongy flavor make rasgulla one of the most famous and loved traditional Indian sweets enjoyed by people of all ages.
