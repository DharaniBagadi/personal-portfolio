Recipe of Chicken Curry

Overview of the Chicken Curry Making Process

1. First, take fresh chicken pieces and wash them properly with clean water. Remove all excess water from the chicken carefully. You can add a little turmeric while washing for cleanliness. Keep the cleaned chicken aside in a bowl.

2. Marinate the chicken using turmeric powder, chili powder, salt, and curd. Mix all the ingredients properly with the chicken pieces. Leave the chicken aside for about 20–30 minutes. Marination helps the chicken become soft and tasty.

3. Place a kadai or deep pan on medium flame and heat oil in it. Add curry leaves and green chilies into the hot oil. The ingredients start releasing a pleasant aroma while frying. This forms the basic flavor for the chicken curry.

4. Add finely chopped onions into the pan and fry them well. Stir continuously so that the onions cook evenly. Cook until the onions turn golden brown in color. Fried onions give thickness and taste to the curry.

5. Add ginger-garlic paste to the fried onions in the pan. Cook it for a few minutes on medium flame properly. The raw smell of ginger-garlic paste should disappear completely. This step gives a rich flavor and aroma to the curry.

6. Put chopped tomatoes into the onion mixture and cook them. Stir continuously until the tomatoes become soft and smooth. Tomatoes help in preparing a thick and flavorful masala. Cook until the oil starts separating from the mixture.

7. Add turmeric powder, chili powder, coriander powder, and cumin powder. Add salt according to your taste and mix everything properly. Fry the masala for a few minutes on low flame carefully. The spices give color, aroma, and spicy taste to the curry.

8. Add the marinated chicken pieces into the prepared masala mixture. Mix the chicken properly so all pieces get coated evenly. Cook the chicken for about 5–10 minutes on medium flame. This helps the chicken absorb all the masala flavors well.

9. Pour enough water depending on the gravy consistency you need. Close the pan with a lid and allow the chicken to cook slowly. Stir occasionally to prevent the curry from sticking at the bottom. Cook until the chicken becomes soft, juicy, and fully cooked.

10. Finally, add garam masala and freshly chopped coriander leaves. Let the curry simmer for another 2–3 minutes for extra flavor. Switch off the flame once the curry is completely ready. Serve the hot chicken curry with rice, roti, chapati, or naan.
