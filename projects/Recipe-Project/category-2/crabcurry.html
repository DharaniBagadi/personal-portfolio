Recipe of Crab Curry

Overview of the Crab Curry Making Process

1. First, take fresh crab pieces and clean them properly with water. Remove unwanted parts carefully and keep the crab aside in a bowl.

2. Marinate the crab using turmeric powder, red chili powder, salt, ginger-garlic paste, and lemon juice. Mix well and leave it for 20–30 minutes.

3. Heat oil in a pan and lightly fry the marinated crab pieces for a few minutes. Keep them aside after roasting.

4. In another pan, heat oil and add chopped onions. Fry until golden brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until the raw smell disappears.

5. Add chopped tomatoes or tomato puree and cook until the gravy becomes thick. Stir continuously until oil separates from the masala.

6. Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, garam masala, and salt. Mix all spices properly and cook for a few minutes.

7. Add water according to the curry consistency you want. Then add the fried crab pieces into the gravy and mix well.

8. Cook on low flame for 10–15 minutes so the crab absorbs all the flavors properly.

9. Finally, garnish with fresh coriander leaves. Serve hot crab curry with rice, naan, roti, or chapati.
