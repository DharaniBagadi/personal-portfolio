Recipe of Egg Curry

Overview of the Egg Curry Making Process

1. First, boil eggs in water for 8–10 minutes until hard boiled. Peel the eggs and make small slits on them.

2. Heat oil in a pan and lightly fry the boiled eggs until golden spots appear. Keep them aside.

3. In the same pan, add cumin seeds and curry leaves. Let them splutter and release aroma.

4. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.

5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until raw smell disappears. Mix well.

6. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft and mushy. Stir until oil separates.

7. Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt. Mix well and cook the masala for a few minutes.

8. Add water and bring the gravy to a boil. Adjust consistency as needed.

9. Add fried eggs into the curry and simmer for 5–7 minutes. Let eggs absorb the flavors.

10. Finally, garnish with coriander leaves and switch off the flame. Serve hot egg curry with rice, chapati, or roti.
