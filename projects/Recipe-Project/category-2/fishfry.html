Recipe of Fish Fry

Overview of the Fish Fry Making Process

1. First, take fresh fish pieces and clean them properly with water and a little turmeric. Remove excess water and keep the fish aside in a bowl.

2. Prepare a marinade using turmeric powder, red chili powder, salt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, and a little oil. Mix everything well into a thick paste.

3. Apply the marinade evenly on fish pieces. Make sure every piece is well coated. Let it rest for 20–30 minutes for better taste.

4. Heat oil in a frying pan on medium flame. Place the marinated fish pieces gently into the oil.

5. Fry the fish until golden brown on one side. Carefully flip and cook the other side evenly.

6. Maintain medium flame so the fish cooks properly inside without burning outside. Add curry leaves for extra aroma if needed.

7. Once crispy and fully cooked, remove fish from oil. Place it on tissue paper to remove excess oil.

8. Finally, sprinkle a little lemon juice and chaat masala if desired. Serve hot fish fry with rice, dal, or onion slices.
