Recipe of Special Gulab Jamun

Overview of the Special Gulab Jamun Making Process

1. First, take milk powder, maida, baking soda, and ghee in a large bowl. Mix all the ingredients slowly and evenly so the texture becomes smooth and soft. Add milk little by little and prepare a soft dough without applying too much pressure. The dough should not become hard because soft dough helps gulab jamuns turn juicy and fluffy after frying. Cover the dough and let it rest for a few minutes so all ingredients combine properly.

2. While the dough is resting, prepare the sugar syrup by heating water and sugar in a deep pan. Stir continuously until the sugar dissolves completely and the syrup becomes slightly sticky. Add cardamom powder and a few drops of rose water for rich aroma and authentic sweet flavor. You can also add saffron strands to give the syrup a royal taste and beautiful golden color. Keep the syrup warm because warm syrup helps gulab jamuns absorb sweetness properly.

3. Now take small portions of dough and roll them gently between your palms to make smooth balls without cracks. Cracks may cause the jamuns to break while frying, so shape them carefully with light pressure. Make all balls equal in size so they cook evenly and become soft from inside. Keep them covered with a cloth to prevent drying.

4. Heat oil or ghee in a deep pan on low to medium flame. The temperature should not be very high because gulab jamuns need slow cooking to become perfectly golden brown from outside and soft from inside. Drop a small piece of dough into the oil to check if the temperature is correct before frying the jamuns.

5. Slowly add the prepared dough balls into the hot oil and fry them carefully while stirring gently. Keep rotating them continuously so they cook evenly on all sides and get a beautiful golden brown color. Frying on low flame is very important because it allows the jamuns to cook completely from inside without becoming hard or burnt outside.

6. Once the gulab jamuns become golden brown and soft, remove them carefully from the oil using a strainer. Immediately place them into the warm sugar syrup so they can absorb the sweetness deeply. Let them soak for at least one or two hours because soaking makes them juicy, soft, and delicious.

7. After soaking properly, the gulab jamuns will increase slightly in size and become very soft. Garnish them with chopped pistachios, almonds, or saffron strands to make the dessert look rich and attractive. The aroma of cardamom and rose water gives the sweet a traditional festive taste.

8. Finally, serve the special gulab jamuns warm or chilled according to your preference. They taste perfect during festivals, celebrations, family functions, and special occasions. Soft texture, rich sweetness, and delicious flavor make gulab jamun one of the most loved Indian desserts.
