Recipe of Special Jalebi

Overview of the Special Jalebi Making Process

1. First, take maida, corn flour, curd, and a pinch of baking soda in a large mixing bowl. Mix all the ingredients properly by adding water slowly until a smooth flowing batter is formed without lumps. The batter should not be too thick or too watery because proper consistency helps create perfect spiral jalebis. Cover the batter and let it ferment for several hours so the jalebis become light, crispy, and slightly tangy in taste after frying.

2. While the batter is fermenting, prepare the sugar syrup in another pan by heating sugar and water together. Stir continuously until the sugar dissolves completely and the syrup becomes slightly sticky. Add cardamom powder, saffron strands, and a few drops of lemon juice to enhance flavor and prevent crystallization. Keep the syrup warm because hot jalebis absorb warm syrup more effectively.

3. After fermentation, mix the batter gently and transfer it into a piping bag, cloth, or squeeze bottle with a small hole. This helps create the traditional spiral jalebi shape while frying. Make sure the batter flows smoothly because even flow gives beautiful round and crispy jalebis.

4. Heat oil or ghee in a wide flat pan on medium flame. The oil should not become extremely hot because jalebis need steady frying to cook evenly from inside while becoming crispy outside. Test the temperature by dropping a small amount of batter into the oil before making the full spirals.

5. Carefully squeeze the batter into the hot oil in circular spiral shapes to prepare jalebis. Fry them slowly while turning gently on both sides until they become crisp and golden orange in color. Proper frying gives jalebis their famous crunchy texture and attractive appearance.

6. Once the jalebis are fried perfectly, remove them carefully from the oil and immediately place them into the warm sugar syrup. Allow them to soak for a few seconds so they absorb sweetness evenly while still remaining crispy outside. The syrup coating gives jalebis their juicy and rich sweet taste.

7. Remove the soaked jalebis from the syrup and place them on a plate. Garnish them with chopped pistachios, almonds, or saffron strands to make them look more festive and attractive. Fresh hot jalebis release a delicious aroma that makes the dessert even more tempting.

8. Finally, serve the special jalebis hot and crispy during festivals, celebrations, breakfast, or evening snacks. Jalebis taste excellent when served alone or together with milk, rabri, or curd. Their crunchy texture, juicy sweetness, and rich flavor make them one of the most loved traditional Indian sweets.
