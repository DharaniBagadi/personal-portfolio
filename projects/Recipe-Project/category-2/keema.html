Recipe of Keema Curry

Overview of the Keema Curry Making Process

1. First, take fresh keema (minced meat) and wash it properly with clean water. Remove excess water and keep the keema aside in a bowl.

2. Marinate the keema using turmeric powder, red chili powder, salt, ginger-garlic paste, and a little lemon juice. Mix well and leave it for 30 minutes.

3. Heat oil in a pan and add the marinated keema. Cook for a few minutes until the meat changes color and becomes slightly roasted.

4. In another pan, heat oil and add chopped onions. Fry them until golden brown. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook until the raw smell disappears.

5. Add chopped tomatoes or tomato puree and cook until the masala becomes thick. Stir continuously until oil separates from the gravy.

6. Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, garam masala, and salt. Mix all spices properly and cook for a few minutes.

7. Add the cooked keema into the gravy and mix well. Add a little water if needed and cook on low flame until the keema becomes soft and flavorful.

8. Cook for 10–15 minutes while stirring occasionally. Allow the curry to absorb all the masala flavors properly.

9. Finally, garnish with fresh coriander leaves. Serve hot keema curry with rice, roti, naan, or chapati
